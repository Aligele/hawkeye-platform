Ward Voter Survey

Kenya · presidential election study
Read to the respondent: “Hello. My name is __________. I am doing a study on how people in this ward view the next presidential election. It takes about 15 minutes. Your answers are anonymous and confidential. We will not record your name and nobody will be able to tell what you personally said. At the end I will ask whether you are willing to be contacted for future polls. That is optional, and any phone number or email you give is kept separately from your answers. Taking part is voluntary, you may skip any question, and you may stop at any time. Do you agree to take part?”
Read each question exactly as written, in a neutral tone. Do not read out candidate names. Never record a name or ID number. A phone number or email is taken only in the optional last section, with the respondent's agreement.
